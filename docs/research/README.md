prior art; architecture docs commit to shipped or planned designs.

## When to add

Landing prior-art surveys, external research notes, or exploratory design
sketches that aren't ready to become architecture docs. Once a research
direction becomes a concrete plan or shipped design, move the relevant
content into an architecture doc or ADR and leave the research note as a
historical reference.
